About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Theoretical Computer Science Group at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Tirupati, working with Dr. S. Raja.

My doctoral research investigated conditional lower bounds, polynomial identity testing, and coefficient extraction problems in arithmetic circuit complexity.

I am interested in algorithms and computational complexity, with broad interests in theoretical computer science.
